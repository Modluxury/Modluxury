The Studio

Content Creation Studio

Some work deserves a room built for it. Our private studio in Toronto was created for exactly that — a professional, controlled space where brands, creators and production teams can work without compromise, and where a vehicle can be presented the way it deserves to be seen.

The studio is prepared before you arrive: seamless backdrops in place, professional lighting set, and generous access arranged so cars and equipment move in and out with ease. Whether the day calls for a full campaign production or a focused afternoon of content, the setting is calm, private and entirely yours for the time you have booked — supported throughout by a team that understands both production and presentation.

  • ✓Studio rental by the hour or the day — booked in advance, prepared before you arrive.
  • ✓Professional lighting and backdrops — a seamless studio setup, ready to shoot.
  • ✓Vehicle-friendly access and space — room to position, light and present a car properly.
  • ✓A private, considered setting — no interruptions, no audience, just your production.
Well suited to — brand campaigns and product launches, creator and influencer content days, dealership and listing photography, and productions that need a vehicle presented at its very best.

Photography & Videography

Photography & Videography

A beautiful subject is only half the work — the other half is the eye behind the camera. Our photographers and filmmakers bring a practiced, deliberate approach to every commission, crafting imagery that carries the same polish and presence as the vehicles and brands it represents.

From automotive features shot in our studio or on location across Toronto, to brand and lifestyle work, event coverage and promotional film, each project is planned with you in advance and finished with care. The result is delivered edited and ready to publish — for your website, your listings and your social channels — so the work leaves our hands complete, not as a folder of raw files and good intentions.

  • ✓Automotive photography and film — vehicles photographed with intent, in studio or on location.
  • ✓Brand and lifestyle shoots — imagery that gives your brand a consistent, elevated voice.
  • ✓Event coverage — launches, showcases and occasions, covered discreetly and in full.
  • ✓Edited, ready-to-publish delivery — finished work, prepared for web, listings and social.
Well suited to — vehicle features and sales listings, brand campaigns and promotional content, social content series, and events where the details matter as much as the occasion.

Automotive Services

Automotive Services

Entrust your vehicle to people who treat it as their own. Our automotive service is the quiet side of MOD LUXURY — the same discipline that keeps every vehicle in our collection immaculate, offered to owners who expect their own cars to be cared for to that standard.

Work is carried out by appointment, so your vehicle is expected, assessed properly and attended to without rush. From scheduled maintenance and servicing to diagnostics, repairs and inspections, you will know what your car needs, why it needs it, and what has been done — communicated clearly by the team doing the work, before any work goes ahead.

  • ✓Maintenance and servicing — scheduled care that keeps your vehicle at its best.
  • ✓Diagnostics and repairs — faults found methodically and put right properly.
  • ✓Inspections — thorough assessments, reported to you plainly and in full.
  • ✓Clear communication throughout — findings explained, work agreed with you first, and a full account of everything carried out.
Well suited to — owners who want one trusted team for their vehicle, and anyone who values a thorough inspection — before a purchase, ahead of a season, or simply for peace of mind.
